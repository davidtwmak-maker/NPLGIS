Landslide Susceptibility of Hong Kong

An XGBoost model on 200 m cells, trained on 19,332 cells that hold a recorded landslide. Terrain, land cover and weathering susceptibility as predictors; 5-fold spatial blocked cross-validation (5 km blocks), ROC-AUC 0.88.

Susceptibility class

    The five classes are equal-area fifths of the model score, so each covers 20% of the land by construction. The columns that carry the information are the landslide rate and the risk against the territory average.

      Factors and weight

      FactorGain %

      Gain share from the fitted ensemble — how much of the model's discrimination passes through each factor. Weathering is derived from lithology: no Hong Kong dataset maps a decomposition grade.
