Trading Card Game Shops in Hong Kong

281 card shops and 49 confirmed places to play, geocoded to the building level with the Hong Kong Government's own location search, then matched against the 2021 Population Census. Where can you go to play a deck with other people, and who lives near those places?

What the map shows

    The choropleth is people aged 15–24 per play venue within 1 km — the crowd most likely to play, against the places they can go. It is a straight-line distance, a proxy for walking, not a routed distance. High values are the underserved areas.
