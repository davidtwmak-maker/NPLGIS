Covered Walkways to School — Kowloon and Kwai Chung

How much of the walk from a public transport stop to each secondary school is under cover? Routes are shortest paths on the LandsD 3D Pedestrian Network — not straight lines. Sham Shui Po · Kowloon City · Yau Tsim Mong · Wong Tai Sin · Kwun Tong · Kwai Chung, 164 schools. Under the 15-minute rule, 107 schools are walk-access to MTR and 57 are bus/minibus-dependent. Each school shows two reference routes — from the nearest bus/minibus stop (the commute) and the worst-case walk from the nearest MTR exit — and a weighted blend (75/25 toward the mode the student would use: walk to MTR under 1,000 m means MTR-heavy).

Weighted distance blends the MTR and bus/minibus routes 75/25 toward the mode the student would use (walk to MTR under 1,000 m means MTR-heavy). Median school: 444 m weighted distance, 7% weighted shade.

Data: LandsD 3D Pedestrian Network (CSDI landsd_rcd_1637222018065_52265, WeatherProof + Location fields) · MTR 3D Indoor Map entry points · Transport Department bus stops and green minibus stops · EDB secondary schools (5 datasets) · Census 2021 District Council boundaries. A path is covered where WeatherProof = 1 or Location = 2 (indoor); WeatherProof = 3 (unclassified) is counted as not covered. The purple dashed line is the worst-case walk from the nearest MTR station exit — the route a student must take if bus and minibus service is out. Median 693 m, 12 min in rain.
