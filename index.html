Festival Walk — 3D floor exits live

Festival Walk (又一城) sits in a valley floor with 495 m of hill behind it. This scene lifts each floor of the official 3D pedestrian network to its own height — MTR level, LG2, LG1, G, UG — so the exits fan out up the slope in the colour of the floor they belong to.

Landslide susceptibility live

An XGBoost model of where Hong Kong's ground fails, on 200 m cells. Terrain, land cover and a weathering-susceptibility factor derived from the CEDD geological map, fitted against 19,332 cells that hold a recorded landslide. Hover any cell to read its score.

LandsD DTM 5 m · CEDD landslide records + geological map · ESA WorldCover · 5-fold spatial CV, ROC-AUC 0.88

Flash flood and storm surge: the two non-blockage mechanisms

Each of the 267 flood-prone locations scored for flash flood (rainfall, terrain, drainage capacity) and storm surge (elevation, distance to the sea, wave climate, the 26 CEDD coastal-hazard areas, nearby pumping stations), on eleven factors from twenty-one named datasets. 215 are flash-flood dominated and 52 storm-surge dominated — but among the 26 coastal areas 19 flip to storm surge, and they sit a median 16 m from the sea at 5 m elevation.

GEO raingauge (1,126,191 rows, 1984–2026) · CEDD wave data (376,191 hourly rows) · HKO tides · DSD drainage network · WSD catchments · LandsD DTM 5 m · CEDD Coastal Hazards Study

Flood-prone areas and the mechanism behind each

All 267 official Hong Kong flood-prone locations from the Drainage Services Department's three lists, each classified by a derived mechanism — local drainage and blockage (185), channel backflow (48), coastal and tidal (32), low-lying ponding (2) — from the coastline, the 5 m terrain model, the drainage network and the catchments. DSD files 226 locations as "blockage", but 46 of them sit beside a major channel.

DSD flood-prone lists (verified 226/226 against DSD's PDF) · DSD channels and inlets · WSD catchments · CEDD coastline · LandsD DTM 5 m

Fishing lights and the stargazing season

Fifteen years of VIIRS night-time satellite data (693 nights, 2012–2026) over the 16 areas where bright-light fishing is permitted in Hong Kong waters. The signal is episodic, not a monthly curve: only about two nights a year carry a fleet, and 13 of the 20 such nights fell in the May–August moratorium window. February, March, August, November and December had none in fifteen years.

VIIRS DNB (SNPP, 693 nights, 2012–2026) · Marine Department permitted areas · AFCD fishing-operation grids · corrected 2026-11-09

Campsites ranked for dark skies

All 41 AFCD country-park campsites scored for night-sky quality using VIIRS Day/Night Band imagery, then weighed against access, facilities and open horizon. The six darkest are in Sai Kung and Lantau South; Ngong Ping is the brightest, and it shows.

VIIRS DNB (12-night median) · AFCD campsites, visitor centres and trails · LandsD 5 m DTM
