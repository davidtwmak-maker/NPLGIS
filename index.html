Hong Kong, in layers.

Every map here is built from official open data — the CSDI portal, Lands Department, LCSD and MTR opendata. The flagship is a 3D view of the pedestrian network that shows which floor you are actually on.

Projects

Festival Walk — 3D floor exits live

Festival Walk (又一城) sits in a valley floor with 495 m of hill behind it. This scene lifts each floor of the official 3D pedestrian network to its own height — MTR level, LG2, LG1, G, UG — so the exits fan out up the slope in the colour of the floor they belong to.

CSDI landsd_rcd_1637222018065_52265 · BuildingID 1108222840

Landslide susceptibility live

An XGBoost model of where Hong Kong's ground fails, on 200 m cells. Terrain, land cover and a weathering-susceptibility factor derived from the CEDD geological map, fitted against 19,332 cells that hold a recorded landslide. Hover any cell to read its score.

LandsD DTM 5 m · CEDD landslide records + geological map · ESA WorldCover · 5-fold spatial CV, ROC-AUC 0.88

Open the web map →

Fishing lights and the stargazing season

Fifteen years of VIIRS night-time satellite data over the 16 areas where bright-light fishing is permitted in Hong Kong waters. The fishing areas brighten from June and peak in September, so the darkest skies in the east and south come in March and April — not during the summer fishing moratorium, which moves the fleets in rather than away.

VIIRS DNB (SNPP, 693 nights, 2012–2026) · Marine Department permitted areas · AFCD fishing-operation grids

See the map ↓

Campsites ranked for dark skies

All 41 AFCD country-park campsites scored for night-sky quality using VIIRS Day/Night Band imagery, then weighed against access, facilities and open horizon. The six darkest are in Sai Kung and Lantau South; Ngong Ping is the brightest, and it shows.

VIIRS DNB (12-night median) · AFCD campsites, visitor centres and trails · LandsD 5 m DTM

3D Pedestrian Network

All 465,892 segments of Hong Kong's 3D pedestrian network, split by the authoritative WeatherProof and Location attributes: which paths are exposed to sun and rain, and which are sheltered.

Volleyball courts

Every bookable court in Hong Kong with live vacancy status, hire charges and annual usage. Peak sessions are 7.7% available against 19.8% off-peak — and 65 outdoor courts carry no price and no wait.

LCSD SmartPLAY feed · refreshed every 5 minutes
