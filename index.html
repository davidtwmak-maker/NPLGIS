Hong Kong, in layers.

Every map here is built from official open data — the CSDI portal, Lands Department, LCSD and MTR opendata. The flagship is a 3D view of the pedestrian network that shows which floor you are actually on.

Festival Walk — 3D floor exits live

Festival Walk (又一城) sits in a valley floor with 495 m of hill behind it. This scene lifts each floor of the official 3D pedestrian network to its own height — MTR level, LG2, LG1, G, UG — so the exits fan out up the slope in the colour of the floor they belong to.

Volleyball courts

Every bookable court in Hong Kong with live vacancy status, hire charges and annual usage. Peak sessions are 7.7% available against 19.8% off-peak — and 65 outdoor courts carry no price and no wait.

LCSD SmartPLAY feed · refreshed every 5 minutes
